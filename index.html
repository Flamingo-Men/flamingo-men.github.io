Top 5 rosters of all time

Each roster is scored on its best lineup (QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, TE, FLEX). A player earns up to 24 points for his positional finish that season (QB1 and TE1 get 24, dropping 2 a spot to 12th; RB1 and WR1 get 24, dropping 1 a spot to 24th) and up to 25 for where he went in our draft the next year (pick 1 gets 24.75, dropping a quarter point a pick to 0 at pick 100). Rosters are the final ESPN rosters for 2020 to 2024, plus the champion and Sacko rosters on file for other years. Kickers and defenses don't count.
