      All-time standings

      Regular season records and points from 2016 through the current season. 2026 counts every completed week, so the totals move each week. Trades include every completed trade on record, 2026 included. Click a column to sort.

      Luck is an official league stat: actual regular-season wins minus the wins your scores earned, where each week you earn the share of the league you outscored (your record if you had played everyone). +2.0 means two more wins than your scores deserved. ESPN weekly scores, 2018 on, completed weeks only.

      Avg pts / season averages finished seasons only, so a half-played 2026 doesn't drag it down. Playoffs counts playoff trips from 2016 to 2025 (2016 and 2017 had four-team playoffs); 2026 joins once its playoffs are set. Avg draft spot is the owner's first-round slot averaged over every draft board 2016 to 2026, scaled to 10 picks: the slot divided by the number of teams, times 10, so 7th of 10 is 7.0, 6th of 12 is 5.0 and the last pick is always 10. Hover a value to see each year.

      * Includes an estimated 2016 or 2017 record. Only Charles's, Duane's and Kyle's 2016 records and Kyle's and Fluff's 2017 records were saved.       The Agora

      Records

      The all-time lists, plus every trade and waiver pickup on record. Pickups come from ESPN for 2018 to 2026. The league wasn't on this ESPN league in 2016 and 2017, so those seasons show trades only.

      League records

      Single-week records and streaks use ESPN box scores from 2018 on (regular season and playoffs; streaks count regular-season games only). Season and career records run from 2016 through the last completed week of 2026. A week still being played never counts.

      Top 10 trade rapes of all time

      Ranked by the gap between what each side got. A player's value is the PPR points he scored for the rest of that season, until he was traded again, minus what a replacement-level waiver player (QB12, RB30, WR30, TE12) would have scored in the same weeks. 2016 and 2017 use prorated season totals.

      Top 5 waiver pickups of all time

      Ranked by the points the player scored in the starting lineup of the team that picked him up, from ESPN box scores (2018 to 2026). In the overall list, running backs and wide receivers count in full, tight ends at 85% and quarterbacks at 60%, because a starting QB is easy to find on waivers and an RB or WR is not. Pick a position to see its top 5 by raw points.

      Top 10 rosters of all time

      Each roster is scored on its best lineup (QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, TE, FLEX). A player earns up to 24 points for his positional finish that season (QB1 and TE1 get 24, dropping 2 a spot to 12th; RB1 and WR1 get 24, dropping 1 a spot to 24th) and up to 25 for where he went in our draft the next year (pick 1 gets 24.75, dropping a quarter point a pick to 0 at pick 100). Rosters are the final ESPN rosters for 2018 to 2025, plus the champion and Sacko rosters on file for 2016 and 2017. Kickers and defenses don't count.
